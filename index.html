Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you work with residential customers?

This offering is focused on small businesses, employee technology, Microsoft 365 and business IT operations.

Can we start with a single project?

Yes. Defined projects can be scoped separately. Ongoing support is available by agreement.

Can TexDesk manage our entire environment?

That depends on your systems, business requirements and support coverage. We assess scope and capacity before committing.

Is on-site support available?

TexDesk is remote-first. DFW on-site engagements may be considered separately subject to availability.

What if administrator access is required?

We arrange authorized access using an agreed secure process. Never include passwords or sensitive data in the contact form.

Do you provide guaranteed 24/7 support?

Not by default. Business hours, response commitments and exclusions are established in an individual agreement.
